Our Story

Boulder's Trusted Bathroom Remodeling Company

Transforming bathrooms across Boulder County with quality materials and expert craftsmanship.

For over 8 years, Boulder Bathroom Remodeling has been the name Boulder County homeowners trust when it comes to bathroom renovations. What started as a small, dedicated team of skilled contractors has grown into one of the most respected bathroom remodeling companies in the region, with more than 500 completed projects and counting.

We are a fully licensed and insured bathroom remodeling company proudly serving Boulder, Longmont, Broomfield, Louisville, Lafayette, Superior, Erie, Gunbarrel, Niwot, Lyons, and the surrounding communities. Every member of our team is committed to delivering exceptional results on every project, whether it is a complete bathroom overhaul, a shower remodel, or a simple vanity upgrade.

Our approach is built on transparency, quality materials, and meticulous attention to detail. We work closely with each homeowner to understand their vision, recommend the best solutions for their space and budget, and execute the project with the craftsmanship Colorado homeowners deserve. From eco-friendly remodels using sustainable materials to full luxury bathroom transformations, we bring the same level of care and professionalism to every job.

Living in Boulder means appreciating quality, sustainability, and design that fits the Colorado lifestyle. That is exactly what we deliver — bathrooms built for Colorado living.

Built on Boulder County Values: Quality, Sustainability, and Trust

Boulder County homeowners have some of the highest expectations in the country — for quality materials, thoughtful design, and contractors who respect their homes. Boulder Bathroom Remodeling was built to meet those expectations. Our team is locally based in the Boulder area and brings deep familiarity with the region's housing stock, building codes, and the design sensibilities that resonate with Colorado homeowners.

We approach every project with the same values Boulder County is known for: durability over disposability, honest communication over sales pressure, and craftsmanship that stands the test of time. We source materials from quality manufacturers and use installation techniques designed for longevity — not shortcuts that look fine at first but fail within a few years.

From the Victorian-era homes of Mapleton Hill to the newer builds in Superior and Broomfield, we have worked in virtually every type of Boulder County home. Our team understands the structural quirks, the permit requirements, and the aesthetic preferences that vary across the county. Whether your bathroom needs a simple update or a complete overhaul, Boulder Bathroom Remodeling brings the expertise to do it right. Call today for your free in-home estimate.
