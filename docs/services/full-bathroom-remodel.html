Full Bathroom Remodel in Boulder

Complete bathroom transformations from initial design consultation to final walkthrough. Licensed, insured, and trusted by Boulder County homeowners for over 8 years.

What's Included in a Full Bathroom Remodel

A full bathroom remodel with Boulder Bathroom Remodeling covers every aspect of your bathroom from floor to ceiling. We handle every detail so you get a completely transformed space that matches your vision and fits the way you live. Whether you are updating a dated master bath or converting a half bath into a full bathroom, our team manages the entire project from start to finish.

Our full bathroom remodels typically include:

  • Complete layout redesign and space planning
  • Plumbing updates and rerouting
  • Custom tile work for floors, walls, and showers
  • Vanity and countertop installation
  • Modern lighting design and electrical updates
  • Fixture upgrades including faucets, showerheads, and hardware
  • New flooring installation
  • Drywall, paint, and trim finishing
  • Ventilation and exhaust fan installation

Our Remodeling Process

Every full bathroom remodel we complete in Boulder follows a structured process designed to keep your project on time and on budget. We believe clear communication and careful planning are what separate a great remodel from a stressful one.

  • Free In-Home Consultation — We visit your home, assess your current bathroom, discuss your goals, and provide a detailed estimate with no obligation.
  • Design and Material Selection — Our team helps you select tile, fixtures, vanities, countertops, and hardware that match your style and budget. We source from trusted Colorado suppliers.
  • Permitting and Prep — We handle all City of Boulder building permits and ensure your project meets local code requirements for plumbing, electrical, and structural work.
  • Demolition and Construction — Our licensed contractors handle all demolition, framing, plumbing, electrical, and structural work with care and precision.
  • Finishing and Installation — Tile, vanity, fixtures, lighting, paint, and trim are installed to exacting standards.
  • Final Walkthrough — We walk through the completed project together to make sure every detail meets your expectations before we consider the job done.

Boulder-Specific Considerations

Remodeling a bathroom in Boulder comes with unique factors that set it apart from other markets. Boulder's older housing stock means many bathrooms have original plumbing and electrical systems that need upgrading to meet current code. The city's building department has specific requirements for permits, inspections, and energy efficiency that our team navigates daily.

We also understand that many Boulder homeowners prioritize sustainable materials and water-efficient fixtures. Our default material selections include low-VOC paints, water-saving toilets and faucets, and sustainably sourced wood and stone options. Whether your home is in the University Hill neighborhood or out near Gunbarrel, we tailor every remodel to fit the character of your home and the standards of the community.
