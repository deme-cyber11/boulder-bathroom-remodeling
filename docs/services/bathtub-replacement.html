Bathtub Replacement in Boulder

Professional bathtub removal and installation in Boulder, CO. Soaker tubs, freestanding tubs, and walk-in models from a licensed and insured team.

Bathtub Types We Install

Whether your existing tub is cracked, stained, or simply outdated, replacing it can completely change the look and function of your bathroom. At Boulder Bathroom Remodeling, we install every type of bathtub to fit your space, style, and budget. Our team helps you choose the right tub for your bathroom layout and handles every step of the installation process.

Popular bathtub options we install include:

  • Soaker tubs — Deep, contoured tubs designed for a comfortable and relaxing bathing experience
  • Freestanding tubs — Statement pieces that stand on their own and serve as the focal point of the bathroom
  • Walk-in tubs — Accessible tubs with a side-entry door, built-in seating, and grab bars for safety and comfort
  • Alcove tubs — Standard three-wall installations that fit into existing tub openings for a clean, efficient replacement
  • Drop-in tubs — Tubs set into a built surround or deck for a custom, integrated look
  • Corner tubs — Space-saving designs that fit into bathroom corners and maximize floor space

Our Bathtub Replacement Process

Replacing a bathtub involves more than swapping one tub for another. Our process ensures that every aspect of the installation is handled correctly, from plumbing connections to waterproofing and finishing work. Here is how we approach every bathtub replacement project in Boulder.

  • Assessment and Measurement — We evaluate your current tub, measure the space, inspect the plumbing and subfloor, and discuss your options during a free in-home consultation.
  • Tub Selection — We help you choose the right tub style, material, and size for your bathroom. We work with acrylic, fiberglass, cast iron, and stone resin tubs from trusted manufacturers.
  • Old Tub Removal — Our crew carefully removes the existing bathtub, disconnects plumbing, and inspects the subfloor and wall framing for any damage or moisture issues.
  • Plumbing Updates — If your plumbing needs updating or rerouting to accommodate the new tub, our licensed plumbers handle it. This includes drain lines, supply lines, and overflow connections.
  • Installation and Surround — The new tub is leveled, set, and connected. We install tub surrounds, tile walls, or panel systems depending on your design preference.
  • Finishing and Cleanup — Caulking, trim, fixtures, and hardware are installed. We clean up completely and walk you through the finished installation.

Surround and Finishing Options

The area surrounding your new bathtub is just as important as the tub itself. We offer a range of surround options to match your style and protect your walls from moisture damage.

  • Ceramic and porcelain tile surrounds in a variety of colors and patterns
  • Natural stone surrounds including marble and travertine
  • Acrylic and solid surface wall panels for a clean, seamless look
  • Wainscoting and beadboard accents for traditional and farmhouse styles
  • Built-in shelving and niches for storage within the surround
  • New faucets, handles, and tub spouts in coordinated finishes
