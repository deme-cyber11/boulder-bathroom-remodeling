Tile Installation in Boulder

Expert bathroom tile installation for floors, walls, showers, and backsplashes. Ceramic, porcelain, natural stone, and glass mosaic from Boulder's trusted remodeling team.

Tile Types We Work With

Tile is one of the most important elements in any bathroom remodel. The right tile transforms your bathroom's appearance, improves durability, and adds lasting value to your home. At Boulder Bathroom Remodeling, we work with every major tile type and help you select the best option for your space, style, and budget. Our installers have years of hands-on experience with all tile materials and installation methods.

  • Ceramic tile — Versatile, affordable, and available in a wide range of colors, sizes, and finishes. Ideal for bathroom floors and walls.
  • Porcelain tile — Denser and more water-resistant than ceramic, making it an excellent choice for showers, wet areas, and high-traffic bathroom floors.
  • Natural stone tile — Marble, travertine, slate, and granite offer a premium, one-of-a-kind look. Each piece is unique with natural variations in color and texture.
  • Glass mosaic tile — Small-format tiles perfect for accent walls, shower niches, backsplashes, and decorative borders. Available in endless color combinations.
  • Large-format tile — Oversized tiles that create a seamless, modern look with fewer grout lines. Popular for bathroom floors and shower walls.
  • Penny round and hexagonal tile — Classic geometric shapes that add visual interest to floors and accent areas.

Our Tile Installation Process

Proper tile installation requires precision, the right materials, and experienced hands. Our process ensures every tile is set correctly, waterproofed properly, and finished to a standard that will last for decades. Here is how we handle tile installation for Boulder County homeowners.

  • Surface Preparation — We inspect and prepare the substrate, ensuring surfaces are clean, level, and structurally sound. For shower installations, we apply waterproof membrane systems to prevent moisture damage.
  • Layout and Planning — Before setting a single tile, we dry-lay the pattern to confirm spacing, alignment, and cuts. This step prevents costly mistakes and ensures symmetrical, balanced results.
  • Thin-Set Application and Tile Setting — We use premium thin-set mortar matched to your tile type and apply each tile with consistent spacing and proper adhesion. Back-buttering is used on large-format and natural stone tiles for full coverage.
  • Cutting and Detail Work — Precise cuts around fixtures, corners, and edges are made with professional wet saws and tile nippers. Every cut is clean and tight.
  • Grouting and Sealing — We apply grout in your chosen color, clean excess, and seal the grout to protect against stains and moisture penetration. Natural stone tiles receive additional sealer coats.
  • Final Inspection — We inspect every surface for consistency, alignment, and finish quality before considering the job complete.

Patterns and Design Options

The pattern you choose has a major impact on the look and feel of your bathroom. Our team can install tile in any layout, from classic to contemporary. Popular patterns we install include:

  • Straight stack (grid) for a clean, modern appearance
  • Running bond (brick) pattern for a timeless, staggered look
  • Herringbone for visual movement and a high-end feel
  • Chevron for a sharp, angular geometric pattern
  • Basketweave for a traditional, woven texture
  • Diagonal layout for making small bathrooms appear larger
  • Mixed patterns with accent borders and feature strips

We also create custom patterns combining multiple tile types, sizes, and colors. Whether you want a simple subway tile shower or a complex natural stone floor with mosaic accents, our installers deliver precise, professional results.
