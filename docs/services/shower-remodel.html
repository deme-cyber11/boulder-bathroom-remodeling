Custom Shower Upgrades for Boulder Homes

Your shower is the centerpiece of your daily routine, and an outdated or poorly functioning shower can make your entire bathroom feel behind the times. At Boulder Bathroom Remodeling, we specialize in custom shower remodels that combine modern design with lasting durability. Whether you want a sleek walk-in shower, a luxurious multi-head system, or a simple refresh with new tile and fixtures, we deliver results that transform your bathroom experience.

Our shower remodeling services include:

  • Walk-in shower conversions with curbless or low-threshold entry
  • Frameless and semi-frameless glass enclosure installation
  • Custom tile patterns including subway, herringbone, and large-format tile
  • Rain showerhead and multi-head shower system installation
  • Built-in bench seating for comfort and accessibility
  • Recessed niche shelving for soap, shampoo, and accessories
  • Shower valve and plumbing upgrades
  • Waterproofing and membrane systems for leak prevention

Walk-In Showers and Glass Enclosures

Walk-in showers are one of the most requested upgrades among Boulder homeowners. They open up the bathroom visually, eliminate the hassle of shower curtains, and create a spa-like experience at home. We design and install walk-in showers in every size and configuration, from compact corner showers to spacious master bath walk-ins with multiple showerheads and body sprays.

Our glass enclosure options include:

  • Frameless glass panels — Clean, modern look with minimal hardware for an open and airy feel
  • Semi-frameless enclosures — A balance of style and affordability with sleek framing accents
  • Sliding glass doors — Ideal for bathrooms where a swinging door is not practical
  • Fixed glass panels — Stationary panels for open walk-in designs that do not require a door

Every glass enclosure is custom measured and installed by our team to ensure a perfect fit, proper drainage, and a watertight seal.

Tile, Fixtures, and Finishing Details

The tile and fixtures you choose define the look and feel of your new shower. Our team helps you select materials that match your design preferences while standing up to Boulder's dry climate and hard water conditions. We work with ceramic, porcelain, natural stone, and glass mosaic tiles in a wide range of colors, sizes, and patterns.

  • Subway tile, hexagonal tile, herringbone, and chevron patterns
  • Natural stone options including marble, travertine, and slate
  • Matte black, brushed nickel, chrome, and brass fixture finishes
  • Thermostatic shower valves for precise temperature control
  • Handheld showerheads for flexibility and ease of use
  • Linear drains and custom drainage solutions
