Vanity Upgrade in Boulder

Custom bathroom vanity installations with premium countertops, modern sinks, and smart storage solutions. Serving Boulder County for over 8 years.

Vanity Styles for Every Bathroom

Your bathroom vanity is the workhorse of the room. It holds your sink, stores your essentials, and sets the design tone for the entire space. If your current vanity is outdated, too small, or falling apart, a vanity upgrade can dramatically improve both the function and appearance of your bathroom. At Boulder Bathroom Remodeling, we install vanities in every style and configuration to match your home and your needs.

Vanity styles we install include:

  • Single vanity — Compact vanities with one sink, ideal for guest bathrooms, half baths, and smaller spaces
  • Double vanity — Two-sink configurations that give both partners their own space in a shared master bathroom
  • Floating vanity — Wall-mounted vanities that create a modern, open look and make cleaning the floor easier
  • Freestanding vanity — Traditional floor-standing cabinets with maximum storage capacity
  • Custom built-in vanity — Made-to-measure vanities designed to fit unusual spaces, alcoves, or specific dimensions
  • Open shelf vanity — Minimalist designs with open shelving for a light, airy aesthetic

Countertop Materials

The countertop is the surface you interact with most on your vanity, and it needs to look great while standing up to daily use, moisture, and Boulder's hard water. We work with a full range of countertop materials and help you choose the best option for your bathroom environment.

  • Quartz — Engineered stone that is non-porous, stain-resistant, and available in a wide range of colors and patterns. Requires no sealing and is extremely durable.
  • Granite — Natural stone with unique veining and color variations. Durable and heat-resistant, though it requires periodic sealing.
  • Marble — A luxury option with classic veining and timeless elegance. Best suited for low-traffic bathrooms with proper sealing and care.
  • Solid surface — Seamless, non-porous surfaces like Corian that resist stains and are easy to repair. Available in hundreds of colors.
  • Butcher block — Warm, natural wood countertops that add warmth to rustic and farmhouse-style bathrooms. Sealed for moisture protection.
  • Concrete — Industrial-modern countertops that can be custom formed, colored, and finished for a one-of-a-kind look.

Sink Options and Storage Solutions

The sink you pair with your vanity affects both the look and usability of your bathroom. We install every type of bathroom sink and help you choose one that complements your vanity style and countertop material.

  • Undermount sinks — Mounted beneath the countertop for a clean, seamless edge that makes wiping down the counter easy
  • Vessel sinks — Bowl-style sinks that sit on top of the counter as a decorative focal point
  • Drop-in sinks — Sinks with a rim that drops into a cutout in the countertop. Easy to install and available in many styles.
  • Integrated sinks — Countertop and sink formed from one continuous piece of material for a seamless, modern look
  • Wall-mounted sinks — Sinks that attach directly to the wall without a vanity cabinet, ideal for very small bathrooms

For storage, we maximize every inch of your vanity cabinet with solutions tailored to your daily routine:

  • Soft-close drawers and cabinet doors
  • Pull-out organizer trays for hair tools and accessories
  • Built-in electrical outlets inside drawers for charging devices
  • Adjustable shelving for tall bottles and cleaning supplies
  • Matching medicine cabinets and mirror storage above the vanity
