Bathroom Remodeling for Erie's Growing Community

Erie is one of the fastest-growing towns along the Front Range, straddling the border of Boulder and Weld Counties. What was once a quiet agricultural community has transformed into a thriving residential area with master-planned neighborhoods, top-rated schools, and a vibrant Old Town district. With thousands of new homes built over the past decade and continued development underway, Erie homeowners are increasingly investing in customizing their spaces, and bathroom remodeling is consistently one of the most impactful upgrades.

Many of Erie's newer homes come with builder-grade bathroom finishes that are functional but lack the quality and personality homeowners want. Our team specializes in transforming these standard bathrooms into customized retreats. From upgrading basic tile surrounds to installing frameless glass showers with premium fixtures, we help Erie homeowners elevate their bathrooms beyond builder specifications. We also work with owners of older homes in the original Erie townsite who want to modernize while preserving the character of their properties.

Erie's location at the intersection of Boulder and Weld Counties means residents have access to the amenities of both regions. As a Boulder County-based remodeling company, we serve Erie regularly and are familiar with the local building standards and HOA requirements common in communities like Erie Highlands, Flatiron Meadows, and Vista Ridge. Call us today for a free in-home estimate.
