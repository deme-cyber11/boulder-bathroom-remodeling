Dependable Bathroom Remodeling in Gunbarrel

Gunbarrel is an unincorporated community located just east of Boulder, known for its quiet residential character and convenient access to both Boulder and Longmont. Nestled along the Diagonal Highway corridor, Gunbarrel offers homeowners a more relaxed pace of life while still being minutes from the amenities of central Boulder. Many Gunbarrel residents have lived in the area for decades, and their homes reflect a variety of architectural styles from the 1960s through the early 2000s, many of which are ready for bathroom updates.

Our team has extensive experience working in Gunbarrel's residential neighborhoods, including Gunbarrel Green, Heatherwood, and the areas surrounding Gunbarrel Hill and Twin Lakes. We understand the unique characteristics of homes in this community, from the ranch-style houses built in the 1970s to the split-levels and two-stories that followed. Each home type presents different plumbing configurations and layout challenges, and our team adapts our remodeling approach to get the best results within each floor plan.

Because Gunbarrel is unincorporated Boulder County, permitting goes through the county rather than a city office. Our team is well-versed in Boulder County building permit procedures and handles all paperwork and inspections on your behalf. Whether you need a simple vanity replacement or a complete master bathroom overhaul, we bring the same level of professionalism and attention to detail to every Gunbarrel project. Call us to schedule your free in-home estimate.
