Bathroom Remodeling That Respects Niwot's Character

Niwot is one of Boulder County's hidden gems, a small unincorporated community tucked between Boulder and Longmont along the Diagonal Highway. Named after the Southern Arapaho Chief Niwot, the town has maintained its historic small-town identity even as the surrounding region has grown. The Niwot Historic District, centered along 2nd Avenue, features charming shops, galleries, and restaurants that draw visitors from across the Front Range. Homeowners in Niwot value this character, and our remodeling work is designed to complement rather than overpower it.

The homes in Niwot range from original farmhouses and mid-century ranches to custom-built properties on larger lots. Each home tells a story, and our approach to bathroom remodeling respects that narrative. We work with homeowners to choose materials, fixtures, and design elements that feel right for their property, whether that means a rustic farmhouse vanity with a vessel sink or a clean contemporary shower with large-format porcelain tile. Our goal is always to create a bathroom that feels intentional and connected to the rest of the home.

As a Boulder County-based company, Niwot is well within our regular service area. We handle all Boulder County building permits for unincorporated areas like Niwot and coordinate inspections on your behalf. Our team has completed projects throughout the Niwot community and the surrounding rural properties. Contact us today for a free in-home consultation at your Niwot residence.
