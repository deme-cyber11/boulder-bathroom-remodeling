Professional Bathroom Remodeling in Boulder

Boulder is where we got our start, and it remains at the heart of everything we do. From the historic homes near Chautauqua Park to the modern residences along the Flatirons foothills, we have renovated bathrooms in every corner of this vibrant city. Our team understands the unique architectural styles found in Boulder neighborhoods, whether you live near Pearl Street Mall, in the University Hill area close to the University of Colorado campus, or in the newer developments of north Boulder.

As a locally operated company with over 8 years of experience, we know that Boulder homeowners value quality craftsmanship, sustainable materials, and designs that reflect the Colorado lifestyle. Our remodeling projects incorporate water-efficient fixtures, low-VOC finishes, and locally sourced materials whenever possible. Every project is backed by our licensed and insured team, and we handle all City of Boulder building permits on your behalf.

Whether you are updating a bathroom in a 1960s ranch-style home near Baseline Road or completing a full renovation in a Martin Acres bungalow, Boulder Bathroom Remodeling delivers results that last. We serve all Boulder neighborhoods including Mapleton Hill, Newlands, North Boulder, Table Mesa, and the surrounding Boulder County communities.
