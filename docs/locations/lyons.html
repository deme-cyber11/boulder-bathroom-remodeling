Bathroom Remodeling for Lyons Homeowners

Lyons sits at the confluence of the North and South St. Vrain Creeks in the northwest corner of Boulder County, serving as the eastern gateway to Rocky Mountain National Park. This small mountain town has a personality all its own, shaped by its stunning red sandstone formations, thriving artistic community, and legendary music scene anchored by events like the Rocky Mountain Folks Festival. Homeowners in Lyons are drawn to the town's creative energy and natural beauty, and they want their homes to reflect that same spirit.

The housing in Lyons includes a distinctive mix of historic cottages in the original town center, mountain-style homes along the canyon corridors, and properties that were rebuilt or renovated following the devastating 2013 floods. Our team has worked with Lyons homeowners through all of these phases, from post-flood restoration projects to thoughtful upgrades in homes that have stood for generations. We understand the specific challenges of remodeling in a mountain community, including working with homes on well water systems and navigating the building codes that apply to flood-impacted areas.

Despite being the northernmost community in our service area, Lyons is well within our regular coverage. We schedule projects efficiently to minimize travel impact and ensure your remodel stays on timeline. Our team handles all Town of Lyons building permits and inspections, giving you a stress-free renovation experience. If you are a Lyons homeowner looking to update your bathroom, call us for a free consultation and estimate.
