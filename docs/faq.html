More Questions About Boulder Bathroom Remodeling

Do you work in homes built in the 1960s and 1970s?

Yes — Boulder County has a large number of homes from this era, and we work in them regularly. Older homes often have galvanized plumbing, original cast-iron tubs, and subfloor materials that require careful handling. Our team knows how to assess these situations and present your options clearly before any work begins. We enjoy working in older homes — they often have great bones and benefit enormously from a thoughtful bathroom update.

Can you match the aesthetic of our home — natural materials, earthy tones?

Absolutely. Many Boulder homeowners prefer a design that complements the natural environment — stone-look tile, warm wood-finish vanities, matte fixtures in brushed nickel or oil-rubbed bronze. We work with our clients to select materials that feel authentic to their home's character, not just whatever's trending on social media. Our design consultation walks through tile samples, fixture finishes, and vanity options in person.

Do you pull permits through the City of Boulder or Boulder County?

Yes — we handle all required permits for your jurisdiction. Projects within Boulder city limits go through the City of Boulder's building department. Projects in unincorporated Boulder County go through Boulder County. We are familiar with both permit processes and manage the applications, inspections, and sign-offs on your behalf. Your remodel will be fully code-compliant when we're done.

How do altitude and Colorado's dry climate affect bathroom materials?

Colorado's dry climate is actually easier on bathrooms than humid climates — but altitude affects some materials like caulk flexibility and certain adhesives. More importantly, the dramatic temperature swings between Boulder's cold winters and warm summers mean that quality grout sealing and proper expansion joints matter. We account for these local conditions in every installation, which is why our work holds up long-term in Colorado homes.
